## Full description (4,000-character limit)

Another Launcher is an Android home screen for foldables and standard phones.

Features:

• Foldable layouts: Layouts adapt when you switch between cover and inner screens, with hinge-responsive motion when supported. On unfolded portrait, choose a centered grid with bottom dock or a wider grid with a side dock.
• Three-Tier Workspace:
  - Page 0 (The Hub): Web search, calculations, weather, and live widgets.
  - Home Pages: 4×6 grids with folders, app and widget stacks, page management, and up to six dock favorites.
  - App Library: Recent apps, smart category folders, and an A–Z list that keeps your choice across folds and restarts.
• Native widgets: Add Android widgets, resize them with edge and corner drag handles, and keep them interactive as layouts change.
• Flexible Search: Find apps, contacts, and Android settings locally; choose Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave, or Startpage for Hub web search.
• Frosted glass: Translucent surfaces, system wallpaper colors, light and dark themes, optional app labels, and notification dots.
• Glanceable Status: Time, battery, Wi-Fi or mobile signal, and device-control shortcuts on side-dock layouts.
• Backup & Restore: Export home pages, favorites, folders, category choices, and settings as JSON.
• Privacy: No ads, tracking, analytics, or cloud account. Weather uses Open-Meteo when enabled; web searches open in your browser.
